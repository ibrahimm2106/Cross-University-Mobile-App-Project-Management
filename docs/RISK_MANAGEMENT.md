# Risk Management

The source report treats the risk register as a tool for identifying, assessing and controlling uncertainty before it becomes a project issue. It emphasises the value of prioritising high-impact/high-probability risks, assigning ownership, planning mitigation and contingency actions, and using the register as a communication tool across multiple universities and external partners.

## Why the risk register matters in this case

The report highlights several reasons the register is important:

- the project has a complex stakeholder environment spanning multiple universities and outside partners;
- participation difficulties and technical integration problems need to be identified early;
- quantitative scoring helps prioritise limited resources;
- mitigation planning allows the team to act proactively rather than reactively;
- risk transparency helps align expectations and build trust among stakeholders.

## Portfolio lesson

For a real delivery project, the next step would be to maintain the register as a live artefact: review risks at governance meetings, update probability/impact after mitigation, assign due dates to actions and escalate risks that move beyond agreed tolerance. Those process improvements are portfolio commentary, not claims about the original submitted report.
