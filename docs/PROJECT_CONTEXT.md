# Project Context

## Case scenario

The project is a **fictitious cross-university mobile application initiative** led by Roehampton Student Union on behalf of participating London universities. The National Union of Students wants to increase opportunities for cross-university social activity, clubs and societies, with a longer-term ambition to extend the concept nationally.

The proposed app is intended to provide:

- day-by-day details of university clubs and societies events;
- links to relevant contact information;
- the ability for students to publicise their own events;
- a shared platform supporting inclusion, diversity and cross-university engagement.

EE is identified in the case scenario as the technical sponsor providing app-development services. The project-management challenge is therefore focused on organising stakeholders, requirements, governance, planning, testing, communication and launch activity so that the development team has clear direction.

## Project-management objective

The portfolio demonstrates how the project could be planned and governed through:

1. a Project Initiation Document (PID);
2. stakeholder analysis and engagement planning;
3. a Work Breakdown Structure (WBS);
4. a detailed delivery schedule;
5. risk-management planning;
6. defined sponsor and project-manager responsibilities;
7. consideration of suitable delivery methodologies.

## Why this is a project-management portfolio

This repository does **not** present a working mobile application. It presents the management artefacts used to plan a complex multi-stakeholder initiative involving universities, students, volunteers, technical specialists and institutional leadership.

## Source boundary

The original assessment brief describes the case study as fictitious and asks the student to take the role of Project Manager. This repository keeps that framing explicit so that the work can be reviewed as evidence of project-management capability without implying that the project was delivered commercially.
